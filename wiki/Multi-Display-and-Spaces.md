# Multi-Display & Spaces Configuration

TabFlow is fully aware of multi-monitor setups and macOS Spaces (virtual desktops).

---

## Multi-Monitor Display Modes

Configure where the TabFlow switcher appears and which windows are included in **Settings > Appearance**:

- **Show on Screen with Mouse**: Switcher summons on whichever monitor your cursor is currently located on.
- **Show on Screen with Active Window**: Switcher appears on the display containing the frontmost focused window.
- **Show on Primary Screen**: Switcher always displays on your designated macOS primary display.

---

## Filtering by Space & Screen

In **Settings > Controls**:

- **Visible Spaces**: Show only windows located on currently visible desktop spaces across your connected monitors.
- **All Spaces**: Show all windows across all virtual desktops and Mission Control spaces.
- **Current Screen Only**: Restrict window items strictly to the display where TabFlow is shown, ignoring windows on auxiliary monitors.

---

## Hidden & Minimized Window Handling

- **Include Minimized**: Minimized windows appear at the end of the switcher list with subtle transparency.
- **Include Hidden Apps**: Applications hidden with `⌘H` remain accessible and switchable.
- **Show Windowless Apps**: Launch or switch to active apps running with zero open windows.
