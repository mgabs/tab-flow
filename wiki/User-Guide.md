## ⚙️ Preferences Overview

Access TabFlow settings at any time by clicking the menubar icon or pressing <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>,</kbd> while the switcher is open:

1. **Controls Tab**: Configure shortcut sets 1 through 4, key delays, release triggers, and blacklisted apps.
2. **Appearance Tab**: Choose switcher layout style, thumbnail sizes, glass materials, and item badges.
3. **General Tab**: Launch at login, menubar icon visibility, and Sparkle automatic software update preferences.
4. **About Tab**: Current version, changelog, and license information.
